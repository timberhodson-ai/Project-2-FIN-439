# Testing an investment decision before committing capital

AI use: OpenAI Codex helped integrate the app, implement the review workflow, run automated checks, and draft this post. The analyst owns the final analysis and decision after review.

This is for education only and does not constitute investment advice.

Could an investment committee retrace a decision if one of its inputs changed? This capstone puts that question into a paper pilot. The app combines an ADP discounted cash flow model with a portfolio dashboard built for a long-only ETF mandate. ADP's valuation sets a price ceiling for advancing to diligence, while the ETF mandate and stress results determine whether the paper pilot should continue.

On the saved snapshots, the ETF portfolio qualifies for a paper pilot with 0% live capital and ADP stays outside the direct holdings. An AI evidence review proposes a diligence question for the analyst to verify against the source material. A passed screen would advance ADP to portfolio-risk testing. The committee can review the source assumptions, change inputs, and see what would reverse the result.
