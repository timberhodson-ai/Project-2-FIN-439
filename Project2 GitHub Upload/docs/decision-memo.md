AI use: OpenAI Codex helped integrate the prior labs, run checks, and draft this memo. The student is responsible for verifying the analysis and approving the committee decision.

This is an educational exercise, not investment advice.

**Proposed committee action for the October 22, 2026 snapshot.** Pilot the process on paper with 0% live capital. Start at 70% VOO and 30% EEM. Across the base case and four reference stresses, paper weights range from 70–80% VOO and 20–30% EEM, with QQQ and VB at 0%. Stop if a stress makes the 7% hurdle infeasible under the mandate or moves any sleeve by at least 15 percentage points.

The process minimizes estimated annual volatility over fully invested, long-only portfolios in five-point increments. It requires a net annual return estimate of at least 7%, at least 40% VOO, at most 30% VB, and at most 30% EEM. The comparison benchmark is 100% VOO. The constrained mix passes with a 12.78% return estimate and 14.99% volatility, versus 14.18% and 15.67% for VOO. Equal weight and inverse volatility both fail the 40% VOO floor.

The 36-month window and the 20% EEM-cap stress each move the allocation to 80% VOO and 20% EEM, a 10-point maximum shift. Risk estimates differ across those runs at 12.17% and 15.09%, respectively. A one-point VOO return haircut and a 50 bp annual cost each leave weights unchanged, lowering the return estimate to 12.08% and 12.28%, respectively. The displayed VOO timing diagnostic is +2.66% from July to August 2026, using adjusted closes in the October 22 extract.

During the pilot, rerun the mandate and all four stresses when fresh completed-month data arrive. Monitor feasibility, maximum sleeve movement, concentration in two equity exposures, and the difference between observed costs and the stress assumption. Revisit any live allocation only after credible out-of-sample results and cost evidence. The estimates come from a single historical equity sample and leave severe downside risk unmeasured.
